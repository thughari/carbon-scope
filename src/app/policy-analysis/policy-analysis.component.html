Methodological Accounting Boundary & Traceability Guardrail

CarbonScope does not fabricate unharmonized regional LCOE costs, localized capacity factors, or speculative NDC achievement claims. Direct operational stack emissions (Scope 1) and global cradle-to-grave lifecycle emissions (Scope 1–3) are strictly separated. Net lifecycle abatement cannot be directly subtracted from a national territorial greenhouse gas inventory without violating international accounting boundaries.
