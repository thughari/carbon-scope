Understanding Electricity Units (TWh)

1 Terawatt-hour (TWh) = 1,000 Gigawatt-hours (GWh) = 1,000,000,000 Kilowatt-hours (kWh). In real-world terms, 1 TWh is enough electricity to power approximately 90,000 to 100,000 average households for an entire year.
